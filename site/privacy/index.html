Who we are

02

Scope

This policy applies only to the LuckyLaunch Android app.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary web request data under GitHub's own privacy terms. The Cudouna Rubanau OÜ organization site sets no cookies and installs no analytics or advertising scripts.

03

Data LuckyLaunch processes

History stays on your device. Sharing your app choices and chances is optional.

The local History feature records the time of a successful dispatch, the destination app's package name and label, and counts derived from those records. It keeps records for up to 30 rolling days and shows at most the newest 50 records.

If you choose Share usage data, LuckyLaunch uses Google Analytics for Firebase to send the package names of the apps in your current saved mix and later saved changes, each app's exact configured chance, and the package name of an app successfully opened from the widget. It also sends pseudonymous information about setup, widget use, launch outcomes, and History screen actions. The service collects automatic app events such as first open, sessions, screens, and engagement, plus app version, device and operating-system information, locale, and an app-instance identifier. Google may process the device's IP address to derive approximate location where applicable; LuckyLaunch does not send GPS coordinates.

Package names can reveal the kinds of apps you use. LuckyLaunch does not send app display labels, picker searches, any individual History record or timestamp, or a full inventory of installed apps to Analytics. It does not track time spent in other apps, direct launches, contacts, messages, photos, microphone, camera, or browsing activity.

Analytics helps us understand whether people complete setup, add the widget, get a successful dispatch, return to the widget, and use History. The app works if you decline or later turn analytics off. LuckyLaunch has no user account, cloud synchronization, or crash-reporting service.

04

Personal information

Consented analytics uses a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. Depending on applicable law, this can be personal data.

Cudouna Rubanau OÜ decides why LuckyLaunch uses Analytics. Google processes this data through Google Analytics for Firebase under the applicable terms. LuckyLaunch does not give Analytics your name, email address, account ID, Android ID, or advertising ID, and does not set a custom user ID. We do not join Analytics data to contact emails or local History records.

To ask about access, deletion, or another privacy right, email the address below. Because analytics has no account or identifying details from LuckyLaunch, we may be unable to match a request to an app instance. We will explain that limit when responding.

05

Advertising

LuckyLaunch shows no ads and does not use Analytics data for advertising.

Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization consent are disabled in the app. LuckyLaunch does not add an ads SDK.

06

Payments

LuckyLaunch processes no in-app payments or purchases.

The app has no billing integration or purchase flow.

07

Data sharing

When you opt in, Analytics data is sent to Google for measurement on behalf of Cudouna Rubanau OÜ. Google may process data in other countries under its applicable terms. We do not sell app data or send individual History records to Google. Read Google's Privacy Policy for more about Google's processing.

08

Retention and deletion

On-device history is retained for up to 30 rolling days. The History screen shows at most the newest 50 records. You can delete it with Clear history.

In Privacy & analytics, turn off Share usage analytics to stop future collection and reset Analytics data on your device. This does not immediately delete data already sent to Google. The Analytics property retains user-level and event-level data for two months; standard aggregate reports may remain longer.

Clearing LuckyLaunch app data or uninstalling LuckyLaunch deletes its local mix, History, and consent choice. Previously sent Analytics data follows the retention and deletion rules above.

09

Security

Android's application sandbox protects locally stored LuckyLaunch data. Analytics data is sent to Google over the network and handled under Google's security controls and applicable terms. No storage or transmission method can be promised to be absolutely secure.

10

Children's privacy

LuckyLaunch is not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has shared data through the app.
