Privacy Policy for LuckyLaunch

Effective date
October 18, 2026
Last updated

01

Who we are

Cudouna Rubanau OÜ develops and maintains LuckyLaunch. You can contact us at ivan.rubanau@gmail.com.

02

Scope

This policy applies only to the LuckyLaunch Android app.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary web request data under GitHub's own privacy terms. The Cudouna Rubanau OÜ organization site sets no cookies and installs no analytics or advertising scripts.

03

Data LuckyLaunch processes

LuckyLaunch collects only non-personal analytics data used for its on-device History feature.

This data stays on your device and is never sent to Cudouna Rubanau OÜ or a third-party analytics service.

The History feature records the time of a successful dispatch, the destination app's package name and label, and counts derived from those records.

LuckyLaunch does not monitor time spent in other apps, direct launches, location, contacts, messages, photos, microphone, camera, browsing activity, or advertising identifiers.

LuckyLaunch has no user account, cloud synchronization, backend service, remote analytics, or crash-reporting service.

04

Personal information

LuckyLaunch collects zero personal information.

This statement refers to external collection by Cudouna Rubanau OÜ or a third party. The on-device History records described above are processed locally and are never transmitted. LuckyLaunch collects no names, account credentials, or profile details.

05

Advertising

LuckyLaunch contains no advertising SDKs and does not show ads.

LuckyLaunch does not use app data for advertising.

06

Payments

LuckyLaunch processes no in-app payments or purchases.

The app has no billing integration or purchase flow.

07

Data sharing

LuckyLaunch does not sell, share, or disclose app data to third parties because app data is not transmitted off your device.

08

Retention and deletion

On-device history is retained for up to 30 rolling days. The History screen shows at most the newest 50 records.

You can delete all history with Clear history. Clearing LuckyLaunch app data or uninstalling LuckyLaunch deletes its configuration and history.

09

Security

Android's application sandbox protects locally stored LuckyLaunch data. No storage method can be promised to be absolutely secure.

10

Children's privacy

LuckyLaunch is not designed to collect children's personal information and knowingly collects none.

11

Changes to this policy

Material policy changes will be published at this same URL with a revised last-updated date before or when the changed app behavior is released.

12

Contact

If you email Cudouna Rubanau OÜ, we use your email address and message only to respond and maintain necessary correspondence. This voluntary contact is separate from LuckyLaunch app data.
