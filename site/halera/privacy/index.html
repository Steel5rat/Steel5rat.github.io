Privacy Policy for Halera

01

Who we are

Cudouna Rubanau OÜ provides Halera. For privacy questions, contact ivan.rubanau@gmail.com.

02

Scope

This policy applies to the Halera Android game. It describes the release build's local game data and how to contact us. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary web request data under its own privacy terms. Our site does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising scripts.

03

Game data on your device

Your Halera progress is saved locally on your device.

Halera stores your sesterces balance, cargo openings and offers, ship appearance, and voyage activity in the app's private storage. The game uses this information to continue your progress when you reopen it. Normal app updates are intended to keep it.

Halera has no account, built-in cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or real-money purchase system. The release build contains no app network permission or analytics or advertising SDK. The game does not send your saved progress to Cudouna Rubanau OÜ or another company. We do not operate a game-data server and cannot view your local progress.

04

Retention and deletion

Halera keeps local progress until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. You can delete it through Android's app settings or by uninstalling Halera. The game has no separate account or remote data to delete.

There is no guaranteed backup or recovery after uninstalling or changing devices. Android backup and device-to-device transfer can vary by device and settings. Some Android 12 or later devices may transfer app data even when app backup is disabled. Do not rely on a restore of Halera progress.

05

Security

Android's application sandbox protects the game's private local storage. Your device's lock and security settings also affect access. No storage method can be promised to be absolutely secure.

06

Contact and requests

If you email us, we use your email address and message to answer and maintain necessary correspondence. This voluntary contact is separate from Halera's local game data. We cannot access or restore the progress on your device. Send privacy questions or requests to:

ivan.rubanau@gmail.com

07

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its last-updated date if Halera's data practices change.
